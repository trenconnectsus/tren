Dr. Shannigrahi is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Tennessee Tech University, where he leads the NGIN Lab. His research covers network protocols, future Internet architectures, 5G mobile networks, and the Internet of Things, with funded projects including Hydra, N-DISE, and TREN.
