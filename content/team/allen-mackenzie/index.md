Dr. MacKenzie is Associate Dean of Research and Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Tennessee Tech University, where he previously served as department chair. His research focuses on wireless communications and networking, dynamic spectrum access, and cognitive radio, along with applications of game theory to engineering problems. He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Cornell University in 2003.
